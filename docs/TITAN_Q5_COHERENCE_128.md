# TITAN Ω Q5: 5-qubit quantum-inspired +128% historical falsification

Goal: 4.15 → 9.462 USDT (+128%) on CLOSED PAPER trades. Never guaranteed.

This is a distinct model from Q4, QSI and H128: five-qubit complex
statevector with explicit price momentum (8/48 bars), trend EMA separation,
volatility compression and candle-body pressure. Complex ZZ couplings on a
five-node ring, RY encoding/decoding, Hadamard interference and Born
majority-state projection. Compute entirely using classical Python CPU; no
quantum computer is used or implied.

Candidate strategies use phase consensus for abstention, breakout/regime
coherence, low-volatility risk gating, counter-trend diagnostic and
phase-zero ablation. Compare the exact same causal features against a
classic logistic model and an independent classical breakout baseline.

The basket is frozen BEFORE historical prices are retrieved: six liquid
publicly listed eligible USDT crypto swaps, selected by a SHA256 ranking of
study ID and instrument identifier. Prior Q4 and H128 baskets and user
blacklist symbols are excluded by code. 90 days of real 15-minute completed
market candles are archived with SHA256 hashes; the instrument
specifications are current public ones, not historical snapshots.

Only the first 40% of timestamps can choose a quantum strategy from
positive regular/stress training results and drawdown threshold. The next
three nonoverlapping 20% partitions and their combined 60% must exceed
+128% net of estimated costs separately, after closed-paper trades.
The replay uses small-account lot/minSz, estimated funding, pessimistic
stop-before-profit ordering, approximate liquidation, ordinary and stressed
execution costs. Avoid treating rare retrospective extreme returns as a
repeatable edge; iterative studies carry substantial multiple-testing risk.

No private data, no order endpoints, no live account execution. The OHLC
prices are real, trade fills and liquidation are approximations. A paper
result, whether negative or positive, cannot prove profitable real trading.
